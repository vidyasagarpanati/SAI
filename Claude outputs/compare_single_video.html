What may be compared, and what may not

Phase durations are in seconds and the true capture rate is measured per video, so they compare across every session here. Joint angles are image-plane projections, so a session filmed from a different position measures a different quantity under the same name; those charts are drawn only within a camera view. Anchor distance is normalised by shoulder width and travels between views.
