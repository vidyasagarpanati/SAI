SCORING RUBRIC (0-10). Apply identically to every athlete and every session.
Do not inflate. Do not lower a score because the athlete appears advanced.

10    No measurable fault. All relevant measures HIGH confidence and stable.
8-9   Minor deviations only, no effect on repeatability visible in the evidence.
6-7   One moderate fault, or several minor faults, clearly evidenced.
4-5   One critical fault, or several moderate faults.
2-3   Several critical faults; the phase is executed but unreliably.
0-1   The phase is not executed in a recognisable form.
null  Only LOW-confidence evidence exists. Set score to null and justification to
      NOT RELIABLY ASSESSABLE FROM AVAILABLE VIDEO.

Every non-null score must name at least one evidence key in evidence_keys.
